Privacy Policy

SP Collection Mail Assistant ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner, Artem Gumashev, with his own Google accounts.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app accesses Gmail messages (read and send) and Google Drive files of the owner's own Google accounts, through the Google APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner read, search and send his own business e-mail and work with his own files. The data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The app does not keep copies of e-mails or files on any server of its own.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
